1. Chicken Tagine with Preserved Lemons and Olives
Description: A savory Moroccan stew of chicken simmered with preserved lemons, onions, olives, and warm spices.
Ingredients:
- Chicken pieces (thighs or legs)
- Preserved lemons, quartered
- Green olives
- Onions, sliced
- Garlic cloves, minced
- Ground ginger, turmeric, and saffron
- Olive oil
- Fresh cilantro/coriander
Steps:
1. In a tagine or heavy pot, heat olive oil and sauté onions until soft.
2. Add garlic and spices; cook until fragrant.
3. Add chicken pieces; brown on all sides.
4. Stir in preserved lemons and olives; add a splash of water.
5. Cover and simmer for 35–45 minutes until chicken is tender.
6. Garnish with cilantro and serve with bread or couscous.

2. Couscous Tfaya
Description: Fluffy couscous topped with a sweet-savory mixture of caramelized onions, raisins, and warm spices.
Ingredients:
- Couscous
- Onions, thinly sliced
- Raisins
- Honey or sugar
- Cinnamon stick
- Vegetable oil or butter
Steps:
1. Cook couscous according to package instructions; fluff and set aside.
2. In a skillet, sauté onions in oil until golden brown.
3. Add raisins, cinnamon, honey, and a pinch of salt; cook until onions are very soft and caramelized.
4. Spoon tfaya mixture over couscous and garnish with toasted almonds.

3. Harira
Description: A hearty Moroccan soup of tomatoes, lentils, chickpeas, and meat, seasoned with ginger, cinnamon, and fresh herbs.
Ingredients:
- Tomatoes (fresh or canned)
- Lentils and chickpeas (cooked)
- Lamb or beef (optional), cubed
- Onions, chopped
- Garlic, minced
- Fresh parsley and cilantro
- Ground ginger, cinnamon, and turmeric
- Tomato paste
Steps:
1. In a pot, sauté onions and garlic in oil.
2. Add meat and brown lightly.
3. Stir in tomatoes, tomato paste, and spices; cook 5 minutes.
4. Add lentils, chickpeas, and water/broth; simmer for 45 minutes.
5. Stir in herbs and adjust seasoning; serve hot with lemon wedges and bread.

4. Pastilla (Bastilla)
Description: A unique Moroccan pie combining savory spiced chicken (or pigeon) with toasted almonds, wrapped in crispy phyllo and dusted with sugar and cinnamon.
Ingredients:
- Chicken breast, cooked and shredded
- Onions, sliced
- Garlic and fresh herbs
- Ground cinnamon and turmeric
- Almonds, toasted and chopped
- Phyllo dough
- Butter, melted
- Sugar and cinnamon for topping
Steps:
1. Sauté onions, garlic, and spices; add shredded chicken and cook briefly.
2. Mix chicken with toasted almonds.
3. Layer phyllo sheets in a pan, brushing each with butter.
4. Add chicken-almond filling; fold phyllo over to enclose.
5. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 20–25 minutes until golden.
6. Dust with sugar and cinnamon before serving.

5. Zaalouk
Description: A flavorful cooked salad of eggplant and tomatoes, seasoned with garlic, cumin, paprika, and fresh herbs.
Ingredients:
- Eggplants, cubed
- Tomatoes, chopped
- Onion, chopped
- Garlic, minced
- Ground cumin and paprika
- Olive oil
- Lemon juice and chopped cilantro for garnish
Steps:
1. In a pan, heat oil and sauté onions and garlic.
2. Add eggplant; cook until softened.
3. Stir in tomatoes and spices; simmer until mixture thickens.
4. Mash slightly with spoon for a chunky texture.
5. Finish with lemon juice and sprinkle with cilantro.
